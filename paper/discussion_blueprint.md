# DISCUSSION BLUEPRINT V2.0

## Overall Strategy: Payoff of the Scientific Discovery
The Discussion section must not merely summarize the experimental results. It must interpret the findings as a scientific discovery about the fundamental properties of aggregate mobility. 

### Narrative Flow
`Experimental Findings $\to$ Scientific Discovery $\to$ Scientific Interpretation $\to$ Methodological Implication $\to$ Practical Implication $\to$ Scope $\to$ Future`

---

## Section Guidelines

### 6.1 Scientific Implications: Recoverable Behavioral Information in Aggregate Mobility
**Objective**: Emphasize the core scientific contribution.
**Logic**: 
`Observation $\to$ Behavior $\to$ Flow`
**Key Insight**: OD is an observable flow. Behavior is latent. The paper successfully recovers latent behavior from observations. Aggregate mobility remains informative because it preserves the dominant behavioral constraints governing destination choice, even after origin–destination identities are removed.
**Physics-Informed Context**: Physical models provide *identification* mechanisms, not just predictive constraints.
**A Conceptual Shift (Section 6.1.5)**: Present the paradigm shift—survey-free mobility modeling should be formulated as behavioral inference rather than flow reconstruction.

### 6.2 Methodological Implications
**Objective**: Highlight the structural design choices.
**Logic**: 
`Behavioral inference $\to$ Component decomposition $\to$ Zero-shot calibration`
**Key Insight**: The fundamental design philosophy is Decomposition. Aggregate mobility serves as *Behavioral Calibration Signals* rather than just incomplete data.

### 6.3 Practical Implications
**Objective**: Outline the real-world deployment benefits.
**Logic**: 
`Deployment $\to$ Data-scarce cities $\to$ Privacy as a consequence`
**Key Insight**: Position PIGF not as a replacement for deep learning in data-rich environments (like DeepGravity), but as a scalable solution for data-scarce and privacy-constrained deployments. Privacy is a consequence of the design, not the core novelty.

### 6.4 Scope and Limitations
**Objective**: Honestly delineate the boundaries of the current study along specific dimensions.
- **Scientific scope**: Behavior is only represented by distance-decay.
- **Methodological scope**: Relies on the Gravity assumption.
- **Experimental scope**: Validated only on US metropolitan areas.
- **Data scope**: Relies on one-dimensional aggregate histograms.

### 6.5 Future Directions
**Objective**: Chart the path forward based on the limitations.
**Logic**: 
`Richer behavioral models $\to$ Physics-informed AI $\to$ Hybrid learning $\to$ Cross-country validation`

### Final Message
**Objective**: End the paper with a strong, memorable vision statement.
*Template:*
> More broadly, this work suggests that the next generation of survey-free mobility modeling may depend less on reconstructing complete origin–destination matrices and more on identifying the latent behavioral mechanisms preserved within increasingly available aggregate mobility products.
